## Geometry and UI evidence

- `SegmentedTimelineAxis` derives its segments from the viewed semester's bound `ScheduleProfile`.
- Consumers: main timetable grid, time gutter and grid boundaries, SCHOOL blocks, MANUAL blocks, long-press inverse mapping, and WeekOverview projection.
- Gaps longer than 30 minutes resolve to 8dp in the normal viewport and 6dp in compact/overview use. The mapping remains reversible inside compressed gaps.
- The official fixture visibly compresses both `12:10–14:00` and `18:20–19:30`; the final teaching range remains `19:30–21:55` and stays inside the viewport.
- Homepage `nowLine` rendering is absent; natural-week state is retained for the conditional return FAB.
- Homepage primary information is `第 N 周` with an inline date range. Semester selection and refresh are absent from the homepage and present in Settings.
- Headers render Chinese weekdays on the first line and `M-dd` dates on the second line.
- WeekOverview is compact, horizontally scrollable, active-only, non-persistent, and uses the shared segmented axis.
- Course cards use compact dedicated typography with title priority and a separate location line; optional fields retain height plus effective overlap-width gates.
- Shared Material vector icons replace Unicode glyphs for navigation, Settings, refresh, overview, and return-to-current-week actions.
- Settings is composed from uniform section and row primitives across the timetable, sync, school-account, and about groups.
- Theme preparation is Daylight/Solid only. No transparent wallpaper feature, image picker, dark mode, or new persisted preference was added.
